Field manual

Recruit: choose a card, then click blue territory. Towers need their full claim radius inside friendly land.

Command: select units, then click the ground. Manual orders override Auto. Scouts explore; Soldiers, Knights and Siege units advance and capture. Archers and Banners hold by default. Auto and Hold apply to selected units.

Resources: towns produce Gold, lumber camps Wood, quarries Stone, and mines Iron. Stay near a site for 3 uncontested seconds to capture it. Sites and castles supply connected friendly territory. Unsupplied towers fight at 15% strength.

Terrain: forests slow movement by 15%. Rivers block troops and supply except at bridges. Mountain passes provide walkable routes through ridges.

Two modes: Classic territory follows units and towers. In War, uncovered land keeps its last owner; enemy claims can still take it. Mountains cannot be claimed. The host chooses the mode for online matches.

Win: destroy the enemy castle. Terrain and resource sites are symmetric. Enemy troops stay hidden outside your vision. Online games use equal income and cannot be paused.
